## SODIUM ION  (`/projects-6`)

# Sodium Ion Batteries
Ideal battery chemistry for energy storage applications.
- Inherently Safer than Lithium
- Abundant raw material, thus Cheaper than LFP.
- No Supply Chain Bottlenecks
- Operating Temperature Range -20 to 60 Deg C.
- Long 10,000+ Cycle life.

### Version 1 (48 V Battery)
> Building a 48 V battery pack using Prizmatic 170 Ah cells with a proprietary
> BMS algorithm designed for sodium battery chemistry. This battery can be used
> for home energy storage, inverters, and residential solar inverters.
> It has 10X the life compared to currently used lead-acid, and unlike Lithium,
> no risk of fire or thermal runaway.

### Upcoming Version 2 (1500 V Battery)
> 1500 V is the standard system architecture for all large solar plants and
> grid-scale storage systems. The V2 will be a high-voltage battery with multiple
> packs connected in series, a high-voltage control box and an energy management
> system.

### Planned — C&I Sodium ESS
> 200 KWh C&I Integrated Sodium Ion battery with Liquid cooling and PCS
